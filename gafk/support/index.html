Requirements

GAFK runs on macOS 13.5 (Ventura) or later, on both Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

How GAFK works

Turn reminders on, pick an interval, and GAFK counts down quietly in the background. When the timer runs out, a notification slides in at the top-right of your screen and fades away on its own — or you can swipe it away. There's nothing to dismiss and nothing to keep track of.

You'll find GAFK in two places: its icon in the menu bar, for a quick on/off switch and a Get Up Now button, and its main window, which opens when you click the Dock icon. Everything is configured in that window.

Common questions

My reminders aren't appearing

Check that the switch at the top of the GAFK window is on — the caption underneath tells you either when the next reminder is due or that reminders are paused. To confirm the reminder itself works without waiting for the timer, use the Preview button, which fires one immediately.

I'm not hearing a sound

GAFK plays your Mac's standard alert sound rather than bundling its own, so it always matches the rest of your system. If it's silent, check System Settings → Sound — both the selected alert sound and the alert volume, which is separate from your main output volume and can be turned all the way down independently. The sound can also be switched off inside GAFK.

What happens when my Mac sleeps?

The timer pauses when your Mac sleeps and picks up again when it wakes, so you won't come back to a reminder that fired while the lid was shut. If you had switched reminders off before sleeping, they stay off.

Does turning reminders off reset the timer?

No. Switching off pauses the countdown where it is, and switching back on resumes from that point rather than starting over.

Can GAFK start automatically when I log in?

There's no built-in setting for this yet, but macOS can do it: open System Settings → General → Login Items & Extensions, click + under "Open at Login," and choose GAFK from your Applications folder.

Does GAFK need an internet connection?

No. GAFK makes no network connections at all and collects no data — see the privacy policy for the details.

How do I uninstall it?

Drag GAFK from your Applications folder to the Trash. Your saved preferences go with it.
