GAFK  for macOS

Privacy Policy

Effective August 27, 2026

GAFK does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. There is no account to create, no analytics, and no network connection of any kind.

Information collected

None.

GAFK does not collect personal information, usage data, diagnostics, crash reports, advertising identifiers, or location. It contains no analytics or advertising software, and includes no third-party SDKs or trackers. This applies to everyone who uses the app, including children under 13.

Information stored on your Mac

GAFK saves your preferences — your reminder message, chosen interval, whether reminders are enabled, and whether the reminder sound is on — using macOS's standard preferences storage. This information:

  • stays on your Mac,
  • is never transmitted anywhere,
  • is not readable by us or by anyone else,
  • is removed when you delete the app.

Network access

GAFK makes no network connections. It does not contact us, and it does not contact any third-party service. The app is sandboxed by macOS and holds no networking entitlement, so it is not technically capable of sending data off your Mac.

Purchases

GAFK is sold through the Mac App Store. Your purchase is handled entirely by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We never see your name, email address, or payment details. Apple provides us only with aggregated sales and download totals, which cannot identify individual customers.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a revised effective date. Because the app collects nothing, any future change would have to come from a change in the app itself — and that would be described in the release notes.
